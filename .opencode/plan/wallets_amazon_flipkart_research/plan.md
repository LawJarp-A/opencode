## Market Trends Identified

### 1. **RFID Protection is a Must-Have Feature**
- Almost all wallets in both platforms feature RFID blocking
- This has become a standard consumer expectation

### 2. **Material Quality Segments**
- **Genuine/Full Grain Leather**: Premium segment (₹900+)
- **Top Grain Leather**: Mid-range (₹400-700)
- **Artificial/PU Leather**: Budget segment (₹189-400)
- **Vegan Leather**: Emerging niche (₹312)

### 3. **Pricing Strategy Differences**
- **Amazon**: Higher prices, fewer discounts, focus on brand value
- **Flipkart**: Aggressive discounting (40-71% off), volume-based sales

### 4. **Review Volume as Trust Indicator**
- Amazon: WildHorn dominates with 19,844 reviews
- Flipkart: Similar trust patterns with established brands
- High review counts correlate with sales leadership

### 5. **Color Preferences**
- Brown (Classic)
- Black (Formal)
- Navy Blue/Olive (Trending)
- Tan/Cognac (Premium look)
