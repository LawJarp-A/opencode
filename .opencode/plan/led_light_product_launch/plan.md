### 2. Competitor Analysis Report

**File:** `.opencode/plan/led_light_product_launch/competitor_analysis.md`

The competitor analysis provides:

- **Market Overview:**
  - India LED Market: USD 4.14 Billion (2024), 7.1% CAGR
  - Key competitors: Crompton, Havells, Wipro, Philips, Syska, Govee

- **Platform Analysis:**
  - Meta Ads: Govee dominates with video/UGC; gaps in emotional storytelling
  - Google Ads: Focus on energy efficiency keywords; gaps in long-tail targeting
  - Amazon: Pricing tiers from budget (<₹200) to premium (>₹2,000)

- **Differentiation Opportunities:**
  1. Emotional/smart home positioning (gap in market)
  2. Gaming segment accessibility (premium pricing gap)
  3. Health/wellness lighting (circadian rhythm claims untapped)
  4. DIY/home decor integration (LED as design element)
  5. Community/UGC strategy (underutilized by established brands)

- **4-Week Campaign Framework:**
  - Week 1: Awareness (30% social budget to video/carousel)
  - Week 2: Interest (retargeting, feature-focused)
  - Week 3: Conversion (70% budget, dynamic ads, promotions)
  - Week 4: Optimization (retention, cross-sell, UGC)

## China Import Considerations

### Shipping Communication

- Standard Delivery: 15-25 business days (ePacket)

- Verified supplier partnerships
