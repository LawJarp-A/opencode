## Market Research Insights

### Amazon India Kanchivaram Silk Saree Market Overview

**Price Range Analysis:**

- **Budget Segment:** ₹437 - ₹619 (Entry-level, semi-silk/art silk)
- **Mid-Range:** ₹849 - ₹1,499 (Popular competitive zone)
- **Premium Segment:** ₹1,939 - ₹2,669 (Pure silk, authentic Kanchipuram)

**Key Competitors Identified:**

1. **SREE KUMARAN** - Rating: 3.8-4.3 stars, ₹899-1,499, Strong brand recognition
2. **Sugathari** - Rating: 4.1-5.0 stars, ₹469-999, High review volume (44+ reviews)
3. **Vardha** - Rating: 3.7 stars, ₹1,939, Premium positioning
4. **Kanchipuram Original** - Rating: 5.0 stars, ₹2,379-2,669, Top-tier authentic

**Market Trends:**

- Growing demand for authentic handwoven products
- Wedding season drives peak sales (Oct-Dec, Mar-May)
- Increasing preference for pure silk over art silk
- Strong competition in ₹800-1,500 range
- Premium segment underserved with authentic products

**Customer Preferences:**

- Primary use: Weddings, festivals, special occasions
- Key buying factors: Authenticity, zari quality, color options
- Average rating expectation: 4.0+ stars
- Price sensitivity: High in mid-range, less sensitive in premium

## Competitor Strategy Analysis

### Top Competitor Strategies

**SREE KUMARAN Strategy:**

- Strong brand presence with multiple variations
- Focus on soft silk/pattu category
- Competitive pricing (₹899-1,499)
- Moderate review volume (8-20 reviews)
- Good rating range (3.8-4.3 stars)

**Sugathari Strategy:**

- Aggressive pricing (₹469-999)
- High review count (44+ reviews)
- Perfect 5.0 star rating on some products
- Strong visual presentation
- Multiple color variants

**Kanchipuram Original Strategy:**

- Premium positioning (₹2,379-2,669)
- Authentic positioning with "Original" branding
- Limited but focused product line
- Premium imagery and presentation
- Strong value proposition for authentic buyers

### Competitive Gaps Identified

1. **Mid-Premium Gap:** Few quality options between ₹1,500-2,000
2. **Brand Story Gap:** Most competitors lack compelling brand narratives
3. **Content Gap:** Limited lifestyle content and storytelling
4. **Email Marketing Gap:** Minimal follow-up marketing by competitors

## Launch Strategy

### Brand Positioning: "KanchiRoyal - Handwoven Heritage"

**Unique Value Proposition:**

- Authentic Kanchipuram craftsmanship
- Pure mulberry silk with traditional zari work
- Competitive mid-premium pricing (₹1,499-2,999)

**Primary:** Women aged 28-50, urban India, wedding/festival buyers
