## Executive Summary

The Amazon shoe market demonstrates strong competitive dynamics with 15 products spanning budget (₹447-₹699), mid-range (₹700-₹1,399), and premium (₹1,400+) segments. Campus Extract leads with the highest rating (4.6★) at ₹1,509, while Bacca Bucci dominates volume with three products in top positions. The average price of ₹952 and median of ₹715 indicate a market skewed toward affordable options, though premium products maintain strong rating performance. Key opportunities exist in the mid-range segment where price-to-rating efficiency shows room for improvement.

## 5. Data Quality Notes & Recommendations

### Current Data Limitations

| Limitation               | Impact                                                 | Mitigation                                           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **No Sales Volume Data** | Cannot calculate conversion rates or true market share | Request order volume data from Amazon Seller Central |
| **No Historical Trends** | Cannot identify seasonality or growth patterns         | Implement weekly data collection for trend analysis  |
| **No Review Count**      | Rating without review count lacks confidence signal    | Add review quantity to future analyses               |
| **No Buy Box Data**      | Missing competitive dynamics (who wins the buy box)    | Integrate Amazon Selling Partner API                 |
| **Single Point in Time** | Static snapshot vs. dynamic market                     | Establish ongoing monitoring cadence                 |
| **No Geographic Data**   | Missing regional pricing variations                    | Request ASIN-level fulfillment data                  |

### Recommended Data Enhancements

**Essential Metrics to Add:**

- Review count and recency
- Sales rank within category
- Buy box ownership percentage
- Fulfilled by Amazon (FBA) status
- Prime eligibility
- Image count and quality score

**Advanced Metrics for Deeper Analysis:**

- Price history (30/90/365 days)
- Rating trend (improving/declining)
- Question & Answer count
- Return rate data (if available)
- Advertising cost and ACOS data

### Analysis Recommendations

1. **Weekly Data Refresh:** Market dynamics change rapidly; establish automated data collection

2. **Competitive Basket Analysis:** Track specific competitor products rather than generic search

3. **Category Expansion:** Include athletic footwear subcategories (running, casual, formal) for granular insights

4. **Customer Review Mining:** Analyze review text for sentiment, common complaints, and feature requests

5. **Pricing Intelligence:** Monitor competitor pricing changes and promotional cycles
