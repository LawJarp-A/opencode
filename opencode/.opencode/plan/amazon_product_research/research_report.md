### Feature Trends

- **Battery Life**: 40-60 hours (key differentiator)
- **Low Latency**: "Beast Mode", "40ms low latency" (gaming focus)
- **Audio Tech**: ENx Technology, spatial audio, AI-ENx
- **Design**: RGB LEDs (gaming aesthetic popular)
- **Charging**: Type-C fast charging standard
- **Connectivity**: Bluetooth 5.3 standard

### Competitive Landscape

- **Dominant Player**: boAt (multiple models across price segments)
- **Gaming Focus**: Growing segment with RGB LEDs and low latency modes
- **Value Brands**: ZEBRONICS, pTron, Kratos targeting budget segment

## Strategic Insights

### Opportunities

1. Gaming audio segment underserved in premium tier
2. App integration differentiator (boAt, pTron leading)
3. Battery life becoming commoditized (need new differentiators)

### Threats

1. Price wars in budget segment
2. boAt dominance limits shelf space for new entrants
3. Feature parity across competitors

### Recommendations

1. Target ₹1,000-1,500 price point with RGB/gaming features
2. Emphasize app ecosystem and brand community
3. Differentiate on design/aesthetics rather than specs
