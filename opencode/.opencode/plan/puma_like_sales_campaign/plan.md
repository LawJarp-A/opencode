# Puma-Like Sales Campaign Plan

## Goal Overview

Develop a comprehensive sales campaign for a new athletic/sports brand that mimics Puma's successful branding and marketing strategies.

**Status:** COMPLETED

**Research Focus Areas:**

- Brand voice, tone, and messaging style
- Visual identity elements (colors, typography, imagery)
- Sales tactics and promotional approaches
- Campaign themes and messaging patterns
- Target audience analysis

**Deliverables:**

- Comprehensive brand research report: `puma_brand_research.md`
- Includes brand voice guidelines with examples
- Visual style recommendations and color palette
- Messaging templates for various channels
- Sales psychology tactics and frameworks
- Campaign approach recommendations

**Key Research Findings:**

- **40% Marketing Investment Increase**: Puma's 2025 "Go Wild" campaign represents their largest marketing investment
- **Gen Z Strategic Focus**: Shift from "win-at-all-costs" to inclusive self-expression messaging
- **Core Brand Positioning**: "Forever Faster" - speed as brand philosophy
- **Visual Identity**: Black (#000000) + Cardinal Red (#BA2026) signature palette
- **Voice Characteristics**: Bold, confident, inclusive, energetic
- **Partnership Model**: Deep integration with ambassadors (J.Cole, Rosé) beyond traditional endorsements
- **Pricing Strategy**: Premium but accessible, 10-20% below Nike/Adidas

## Campaign Framework (Based on Puma Research)

### Phase 1: Foundation Building (Months 1-3)

- Establish core brand messaging and visual identity
- Develop consistent voice across all channels
- Create asset library for rapid deployment
- Build audience through organic content

### Phase 2: Awareness Campaigns (Months 4-8)

- Launch first major brand campaign
- Activate ambassador partnerships
- Increase paid media investment
- Focus on reach and recognition metrics

### Phase 3: Engagement Campaigns (Months 9-14)

- Deepen audience engagement through interactive content
- Expand product-specific campaigns
- Build loyalty program enrollment
- Focus on engagement and consideration metrics

### Phase 4: Conversion Campaigns (Months 15-20)

- Drive direct response through targeted offers
- Optimize conversion pathways
- Expand retail partner activations
- Focus on sales and efficiency metrics
