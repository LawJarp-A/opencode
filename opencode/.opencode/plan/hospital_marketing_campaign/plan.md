### Unit 2: Marketing Strategy Research

**Status:** ✅ COMPLETE

**Research Conducted:**

- Current healthcare marketing trends and digital transformation
- HIPAA marketing rules and compliance requirements
- Recommended marketing channels and tactics
- Industry benchmarks and success metrics
- Successful hospital marketing case studies

**Report Generated:** marketing_strategy_research.md

**Key Findings:**

- Digital-first approach essential: 72% of healthcare leaders believe digital platform investment is critical
- Healthcare PPC CTRs: 2.5-3.5%, higher than most industries
- Email open rates: 22-25%, above cross-industry average
- Notable ROI: Health system orthopedic campaigns achieving 14:1 return
- HIPAA requires written authorization for PHI use in marketing
- Community-focused campaigns (NewYork-Presbyterian, Maimonides) highly effective

**Strategic Recommendations:**

- Digital advertising allocation: 30-40% of budget
- Content marketing: 15-20%
- Multi-channel integration: digital, community, physician referrals
- HIPAA compliance integration in all marketing activities
- Four-phase campaign approach: Foundation → Development → Execution → Scale
