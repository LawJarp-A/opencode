# Comprehensive Guide to Launching a Book on Amazon India

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive guide provides detailed information for authors, publishers, and booksellers looking to launch and sell books on Amazon India. The document covers all essential requirements including seller account setup, book listing procedures, compliance regulations, fulfillment options, fee structures, and realistic timeline expectations for a successful book launch on India's largest online marketplace.

Amazon India represents a massive opportunity for book sellers, with over 28 million books sold to customers across the country, averaging 70,000 books per day and 3,000 books every hour. The platform offers a tax-exempt category for books, making it particularly attractive for new sellers entering the e-commerce space.

## 1. Amazon Seller Account Requirements

### 1.1 Account Types: Individual vs Professional

Amazon India offers two types of seller accounts, each designed for different selling volumes and business needs.

**Individual Seller Account:**

- **Cost:** No monthly subscription fee
- **Fee per item:** ₹40 per item sold (approximately $0.50)
- **Best for:** Sellers with fewer than 35 items per month
- **Limitations:** Cannot use bulk listing tools, limited access to advertising features, no access to Amazon API
- **Registration:** Minimal documentation required

**Professional Seller Account:**

- **Cost:** ₹1,000 + GST per month (approximately $12-15)
- **Fee per item:** No per-item fee
- **Best for:** Sellers planning to list more than 35 items monthly
- **Benefits:** Full access to all selling tools, bulk listing capabilities, advertising programs, API access, Amazon Brand Registry eligibility
- **Recommendation:** For book launches with multiple titles or expected volume, the Professional account is more cost-effective

**Key Consideration:** For most book launches, the Professional seller account is recommended because it provides access to advanced features essential for competitive positioning, including the ability to create enhanced brand content, access advertising platforms, and use bulk inventory management tools.

### 1.2 Registration Process

The registration process for Amazon India seller account follows these systematic steps:

**Step 1: Account Creation**

- Visit sell.amazon.in and click "Start Selling"
- Provide business email address and create password
- Enter verification code sent to email

**Step 2: Business Information**

- Business name (as it will appear to customers)
- Business address and contact details
- Store name for your Amazon presence
- Primary contact person information

**Step 3: GST Registration Details**

- GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number)
- GST registration certificate upload
- Important note: Books are generally GST-exempt, but GST registration is still required for seller account setup

**Step 4: Product Category Selection**

- Select "Books" as primary category
- May require additional verification for certain book types
- Accept seller agreement terms and conditions

**Step 5: Billing and Banking**

- Credit card for verification purposes
- Bank account details for payouts
- Bank account verification process (may take 3-7 days)
- IFSC code, account number, and account holder name

**Step 6: Identity Verification**

- PAN card details (mandatory for Indian sellers)
- Aadhaar card or other government-issued ID
- Business registration documents if applicable
- Verification call from Amazon team

### 1.3 Required Documents

**Essential Documents for Registration:**

| Document Type             | Requirement | Notes                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **GSTIN**                 | Mandatory   | Even though books are GST-exempt, GST registration is required     |
| **PAN Card**              | Mandatory   | Personal PAN for individual sellers, Business PAN for companies    |
| **Bank Account**          | Mandatory   | Active account with IFSC code, must match registered business name |
| **Address Proof**         | Required    | Utility bill, rent agreement, or bank statement (within 3 months)  |
| **Identity Proof**        | Required    | Aadhaar card, passport, or driver's license                        |
| **Business Registration** | Conditional | Required if registering as Private Limited, Partnership, or LLP    |

**For Authors Self-Publishing:**

- Individual PAN card is primary requirement
- No business registration necessary if selling under personal name
- Bank account must match PAN card name

**For Publishers:**

- Company PAN card
- GST registration under company name
- Authorisation letter from company for selling account
- Board resolution if applicable

### 1.4 Verification Process

The verification process involves multiple layers to ensure seller legitimacy:

**Document Verification (24-72 hours):**

- Automated system checks submitted documents
- Manual review if automated verification fails
- Status updates visible in Seller Central dashboard

**Phone Verification:**

- Amazon calls registered phone number
- May ask basic business questions
- Must be answered by account holder

**Video Verification (occasionally required):**

- Scheduled video call with Amazon representative
- Shows original documents
- Confirms identity and business legitimacy

**Account Approval Timeline:**

- **Standard cases:** 24-48 hours if all documents clear
- **Complex cases:** 5-7 days for additional verification
- **Problem cases:** Up to 2 weeks or more
- **Average new seller approval:** 3-5 days

## 2. Book Listing Requirements

### 2.1 ISBN Requirements

**Understanding ISBN vs ASIN:**

The International Standard Book Number (ISBN) and Amazon Standard Identification Number (ASIN) serve different purposes in the book selling ecosystem.

**ISBN Requirements for Amazon India:**

| Scenario                 | ISBN Requirement                    | Notes                                |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **New commercial books** | Required                            | Must have valid 13-digit ISBN        |
| **Self-published books** | Required if selling physical copies | Obtain from ISBN India (isbn.gov.in) |
| **Used books**           | Required                            | Must match actual book ISBN          |
| **Bulk/combo packs**     | Required                            | Each unique product needs ISBN       |
| **Kindle eBooks**        | Not required for KDP                | Separate ASIN created automatically  |

**ISBN Acquisition in India:**

- Raja Rammohun Roy National Agency for ISBN (isbn.gov.in)
- Free ISBN allocation for Indian publishers and authors
- Process takes 2-4 weeks for new applications
- ISBN is unique to each edition and format (print vs ebook require separate ISBNs)

**Common ISBN Issues on Amazon India:**

- ISBN format errors (must be 13 digits, properly formatted)
- ISBN not matching actual book (causes listing rejections)
- Multiple books sharing same ISBN (violation of Amazon policies)
- ISBN agency verification failures (system sometimes miscategorizes books)

**ASIN Creation Process:**
When a book doesn't exist in Amazon's catalog, sellers create a new ASIN using the ISBN as the base identifier. The process involves:

1. Enter ISBN in product search
2. System indicates "No exact match found"
3. Click "Create new ASIN" option
4. Fill comprehensive product details
5. Submit for Amazon review (24-72 hours)

### 2.3 Listing Optimization Requirements

**Essential Listing Elements:**

**Product Title:**

- Maximum 200 characters (though Amazon displays first 80-100)
- Must include: Book title, Author name, Edition (if applicable)
- Recommended format: Title | Author | Edition | Publisher
- Avoid: ALL CAPS, promotional phrases, ASIN numbers

**Product Description:**

- Detailed book synopsis (500-2000 characters)
- Include plot summary, key topics, or book highlights
- Format with paragraphs and bullet points for readability
- Avoid excessive promotional language

**Author Information:**

- Full author name (appear as byline)
- Brief author bio (recommended but not mandatory)
- Author profile links (optional)
- Multiple authors: list primary author first

**Bullet Points/Key Features:**

- Format: 5-7 bullet points maximum
- Include: Book format, page count, language, special features
- Highlight unique selling propositions
- Use keyword-rich descriptions

**Product Images:**

- Primary image: High-resolution book cover (minimum 1000x1000 pixels)
- Additional images: Back cover, spine, sample pages, author photo
- White background preferred for primary image
- No text, watermarks, or promotional overlays on cover images
- File format: JPEG, PNG, or GIF

**A+ Content (Enhanced Brand Content):**

- Available for Brand Registry enrolled sellers
- Enhanced visual storytelling
- Comparison charts, detailed specifications
- Can increase conversion rates by 10-15%

## 4. Fulfillment Options

### 4.1 Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA)

FBA is Amazon's comprehensive fulfillment service where Amazon handles storage, packing, shipping, and customer service.

**How FBA Works for Books:**

1. Seller creates listings in Seller Central
2. Books are shipped to Amazon fulfillment centers
3. Amazon stores inventory in their warehouses
4. When customer places order, Amazon picks, packs, and ships
5. Amazon handles customer service and returns

**FBA Benefits:**

- **Prime Badge:** Eligible for Prime shipping, increasing conversion
- **Customer Trust:** Amazon handles fulfillment quality
- **Time Savings:** No manual shipping or tracking
- **Customer Service:** Amazon handles inquiries and returns
- **Multi-Channel Fulfillment:** Can fulfill orders from other platforms
- **Storage Optimization:** Amazon manages inventory across centers

**FBA Requirements for Books:**

| Requirement        | Specification                          | Notes                            |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Book Condition** | New only for FBA                       | Used books not eligible          |
| **Packaging**      | Must meet Amazon standards             | See FBA product prep guide       |
| **Barcode**        | Amazon barcode or manufacturer barcode | Amazon applies labels            |
| **Quantity**       | Minimum 1 unit                         | No maximum limit                 |
| **Size**           | Standard book sizes                    | Oversize may have different fees |

**FBA Inventory Requirements:**

- Products must be new and in sellable condition
- Proper packaging to prevent damage
- Amazon barcode labels on each unit
- FNSKU label generation in Seller Central
- Inbound shipping plan creation

**FBA Storage Centers:**

- Multiple fulfillment centers across India
- Strategic placement for faster delivery
- Inventory can be split across centers
- Amazon manages inventory distribution

### 4.2 Self-Fulfillment (FBM)

Self-fulfillment means the seller handles all storage, packing, and shipping independently.

**When to Choose FBM:**

- Low volume sales
- Higher profit margins on individual books
- Custom packaging requirements
- Used or collectible books
- Books requiring special handling

**FBM Requirements:**

| Aspect           | Requirement                           | Notes                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Storage**      | Own facility or third-party warehouse | Must maintain inventory      |
| **Packing**      | Seller responsibility                 | Must ensure product safety   |
| **Shipping**     | Seller arranges delivery              | Use Amazon-shipping partners |
| **Tracking**     | Must provide tracking number          | Required within 24 hours     |
| **Delivery SLA** | 3-7 business days standard            | Extended for remote areas    |

**Self-Shipping Requirements:**

- Maintain adequate inventory levels
- Pack books securely to prevent damage
- Ship within 24 hours of order confirmation
- Provide accurate tracking information
- Handle customer inquiries directly
- Manage returns processing

**Amazon Shipping Partners:**

- Amazon Easy Ship (for select sellers)
- Third-party logistics providers
- Direct carrier partnerships
- Must use Amazon-approved shipping methods

### 4.3 Easy Ship

Easy Ship is an Amazon-managed service where Amazon provides shipping labels but seller handles packing and handover.

**Easy Ship Features:**

- Amazon generates shipping labels
- Free pickup from seller's location
- Cash on delivery available
- Real-time tracking
- Handling of customer issues

**Easy Ship Eligibility:**

- Active Professional seller account
- Products must meet size and weight criteria
- Located within Amazon serviceable areas
- Performance metrics meet requirements

**Book-Specific Considerations:**

- Standard book sizes qualify easily
- Weight typically under 1kg for most books
- Cost-effective for medium volume sellers
- Simplified logistics management

### 4.4 Storage Requirements

**FBA Storage Requirements:**

| Storage Aspect        | Requirement                 | Notes                                |
| --------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Temperature**       | Ambient storage             | No special requirements for books    |
| **Humidity**          | Controlled environment      | Books sensitive to moisture          |
| **Fire Safety**       | Standard warehouse safety   | No hazardous materials nearby        |
| **Inventory Limits**  | Based on seller performance | New sellers may have limits          |
| **Long-term Storage** | Fees apply after 365 days   | Slow-moving inventory considerations |

**Self-Storage Best Practices:**

- Clean, dry, pest-free environment
- Temperature controlled (avoid extreme heat/cold)
- Proper shelving to prevent damage
- Inventory management system
- Regular stock rotation
- Security measures for valuable inventory

## 5. Fee Structure

### 5.1 Referral Fees

Referral fees are Amazon's commission on each sale, calculated as a percentage of the total selling price.

**Book Category Referral Fees (2024-2025):**

| Product Type                    | Referral Fee         | Notes                                   |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Books (General)**             | 15% of selling price | Most common category rate               |
| **Academic/Professional Books** | 15% of selling price | Higher for specialized content          |
| **Children's Books**            | 15% of selling price | May have different rates for some items |
| **eBooks**                      | 30% through KDP      | Different platform, different fees      |

**Referral Fee Calculation:**

```
Total Referral Fee = Item Selling Price × Referral Fee Percentage
```

**Example:**

- Book selling price: ₹500
- Referral fee (15%): ₹75
- Amount after referral: ₹425 (before other fees)

**Minimum Referral Fee:**

- Some categories have minimum referral fees
- Books generally do not have minimum fees
- Check current fee schedule for exact figures

### 5.2 Closing Fees

Closing fees are fixed fees per order based on the item price and fulfillment method.

**FBA Closing Fees:**

| Price Range   | FBA Closing Fee | Notes              |
| ------------- | --------------- | ------------------ |
| Under ₹250    | ₹15             | For light items    |
| ₹250 - ₹500   | ₹25             | Standard rate      |
| ₹500 - ₹1,000 | ₹35             | Mid-range items    |
| Above ₹1,000  | ₹40+            | Higher value items |

**FBM Closing Fees:**

- Typically lower than FBA closing fees
- ₹5-15 range for most books
- Varies by price point and service level

**Closing Fee Calculation:**

- Applied per order, not per item
- Added to referral fee
- Combined with shipping costs
- GST applicable on closing fees

### 5.3 Shipping Fees

**FBA Weight Handling Fees (2024-2025):**

| Weight     | Standard Shipping | Expedited Shipping  |
| ---------- | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Up to 500g | ₹29-40            | ₹40-55              |
| 500g - 1kg | ₹40-55            | ₹55-75              |
| 1kg - 2kg  | ₹55-75            | ₹75-100             |
| 2kg+       | ₹75-100+          | Based on dimensions |

**FBM Shipping Costs:**

- Seller's responsibility
- Use Amazon shipping partners for discounted rates
- Factor into product pricing
- Cash on delivery available through Amazon

**Easy Ship Fees:**

- Commission-based pricing
- Per-order fees
- Includes pickup and delivery
- Discounted rates for high-volume sellers

### 5.4 Other Applicable Charges

**FBA Storage Fees:**

- Monthly storage charges based on cubic feet
- Higher during peak season (Oct-Dec)
- Long-term storage fees after 365 days
- Calculated monthly

**Pick and Pack Fees:**

- Per-unit handling fee for FBA
- Varies by book size and weight
- Included in weight handling calculations

**GST on Amazon Fees:**

- 18% GST on all Amazon fees
- Input Tax Credit available for registered businesses
- Must be factored into pricing

**Optional Services:**

- **Advertising:** CPC campaigns, sponsored products
- **A+ Content:** Enhanced listing content creation
- **Brand Registry:** Annual subscription for brand protection
- **Subscription Services:** Amazon Prime, Business programs
