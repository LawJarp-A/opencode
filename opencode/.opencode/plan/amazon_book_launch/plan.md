### Summary of Research Findings

#### 1. Seller Account Requirements

- **Two account types:** Individual (₹40/item) vs Professional (₹1,000/month)
- **Required documents:** GSTIN, PAN card, bank account, identity proof, address proof
- **Timeline:** 3-10 days average (can be expedited to 2 days with proper documentation)
- **Books are GST-exempt category but GST registration still required for seller account**

#### 2. Book Listing Requirements

- **ISBN required** for physical books (13-digit format)
- **ASIN created automatically** using ISBN as base identifier
- **Category selection** from extensive list including English, Hindi, Regional languages, Academic, Children's books
- **Listing optimization** requirements: high-resolution images (1000x1000px), detailed descriptions, keyword-rich content

#### 3. Compliance & Legal Requirements

- **Publisher details** (name, publication date, edition)
- **Author information** (full legal name, bio recommended)
- **Content guidelines** prohibiting illegal content, copyright infringement, hate speech
- **Pricing regulations** requiring MRP display, allowing discount pricing

#### 4. Fulfillment Options

- **FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon):** Best for Prime eligibility, Amazon handles storage/shipping/customer service
- **Self-fulfillment (FBM):** Seller handles all logistics, best for used/collectible books
- **Easy Ship:** Amazon provides labels, seller handles packing and pickup

#### 5. Fee Structure

- **Referral fee:** 15% of selling price for books
- **Closing fee:** ₹5-40 per order based on price range
- **FBA shipping:** ₹29-75 based on weight
- **Storage fees:** Monthly charges based on cubic feet
- **18% GST** on all Amazon fees

#### 6. Timeline Expectations

- **Account setup:** 3-10 days average
- **Listing approval:** 1-7 days per book
- **First sale:** 5-30 days (60% of new sellers sell within 4 weeks)
- **Consistent sales:** 4-8 weeks typically

### Key Statistics from Research

- **60%** of new sellers get first sale within 4 weeks
