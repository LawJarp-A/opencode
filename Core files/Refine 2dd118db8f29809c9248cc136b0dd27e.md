# 📋 SHAPE UP PITCH

## Problem

AI generates at 90-95%. Brands need 100%. The last 5-10% is the difference between "good enough" and "excellent."

*The AI Middle Trap:*

```
Brand generates 20 ad creatives with AI (10 minutes)
  ↓
Review: "These are... good. Not great."
  ↓
The choice:
  Option A: Use as-is (fast but compromises brand)
  Option B: Manually edit all 20 (eliminates AI speed benefit)
  Option C: Hire agency ($5K-20K, 2-4 weeks)
  ↓
Result: Can't scale excellence. AI stays at 90% forever.
```

*Real user pain:* "I generated 30 product images for our hero campaign. They're 90% there, but we're spending $10K on Meta ads. I can't risk 'almost perfect' creatives. I need human polish but agencies take 3 weeks. I'm stuck."

*The cost:*

- Underperforming campaigns (good loses to great)
- Manual editing bottleneck (team becomes QA, not creators)
- No learning (AI never improves)
- False choice (speed OR excellence, never both)

## Appetite

*1 week*

Refine™ solves the "why would I use AI if I still need humans?" objection. It's the answer to "this is 90% but I need 100%." Without Refine™, users hit the ceiling and leave.

We're investing 1 week because the core is simple: route request → expert edits → return output. The magic is the learning loop (RL feedback), not complex infrastructure.

## Solution

*Refine™ = Expert Polish + Learning Loop*

Not just human editing. *Human editing that teaches the AI.*

*The Flow:*

```
AI generates 20 creatives (10 minutes, 90% quality)
  ↓
User flags 3 for hero campaign: "Need 100% for $10K ad spend"
  ↓
Refine™ matches to expert (Sarah, apparel specialist, 4.8★)
  ↓
Expert receives:
  - Original images
  - Brand context (auto-loaded from Brand Memory)
  - User instructions
  - Reference library (top performers)
  ↓
Expert edits (3.5 hours):
  - Color correction (+15% warmth)
  - Composition (tighter crop, rule of thirds)
  - Brand consistency check
  ↓
User approves ✓
  ↓
RL model analyzes edits:
  "User's brand prefers warm tones, tight crops, high contrast"
  ↓
Next AI generation starts at 93% instead of 90%
  ↓
Week 12: AI at 95%, refinement needs drop 40%
```

*The Compounding Effect:*

```
Week 1: AI at 90% → 20% of outputs need refinement → $600 cost
Week 12: AI at 93% → 12% need refinement → $360 cost
Week 52: AI at 96% → 5% need refinement → $150 cost

ROI: Refinement pays for itself by improving the AI
```

*Key Features:*

1. *Selective refinement:* Flag specific outputs (not everything)
2. *Expert matching:* Route to specialist (apparel, home decor, tech, etc.)
3. *Brand context auto-load:* Experts get guidelines, past campaigns, palette
4. *Fast turnaround:* Standard (24h) or Rush (4h, +50% cost)
5. *Unlimited revisions:* Within scope, until approved
6. *RL feedback loop:* Every edit trains the AI

*Pricing tiers:*

- *Quick Polish:* $50-150 (color, crop, minor fixes)
- *Creative Direction:* $200-500 (composition, brand consistency, major edits)
- *Full Production:* $500-1000 (complex work, multiple assets)

## Rabbit Holes

*Don't build:*

- *Expert marketplace/bidding.* Fixed matching algorithm, no auction system.
- *Real-time collaboration.* Async only. Expert works offline, uploads when done.
- *Custom expert profiles.* Pre-vetted pool, we handle onboarding.
- *Payment disputes.* Fixed pricing, no negotiation or chargebacks.
- *Complex SLA tiers.* Two options only: Standard (24h) and Rush (4h).
- *Expert rating system UX.* Simple 5-star, no reviews/comments for Phase 1.

*Reuse existing:*

- Files library (store inputs/outputs)
- Brand Memory (provide context to experts)
- Version history (track edits)

*Watch out for:*

- Expert availability (need backup experts per category)
- Quality control (bad expert work damages trust)
- Scope creep ("unlimited revisions" must have boundaries)
- Learning loop quality (bad edits shouldn't train AI)

## No-Gos

*Explicitly NOT doing:*

- ✖ Expert marketplace (users can't choose/bid on experts)
- ✖ Expert profiles visible to users (black box matching)
- ✖ Real-time chat with experts (async communication only)
- ✖ Payment negotiation (fixed pricing tiers)
- ✖ Custom SLA (only Standard 24h or Rush 4h)
- ✖ Expert reviews/comments (Phase 2)

# 🔧 ENGINEERING PRD

## 1. OBJECTIVE

*Purpose:*
Build a human expert layer that polishes AI outputs to 100% quality while feeding edits back to the AI for continuous improvement. Close the 90-95% gap and create a learning loop that reduces refinement needs over time.

*Goal:*

- Enable users to achieve 100% quality outputs for high-stakes work
- Turnaround: 24 hours (standard) or 4 hours (rush)
- Quality: 90%+ approval rate on first delivery
- Learning: AI quality improves measurably within 3 months
- Economics: Refinement becomes less needed as AI improves

*Context:*
Refine™ answers the "AI can't do everything" objection. It's the safety net for critical work and the training ground for AI improvement.

## 2. SCOPE

### In Scope

*Core Refinement Flow:*

- User selection (flag outputs from Spaces/Plans/Files for refinement)
- Request form (instructions, urgency, context)
- Expert matching (category, availability, rating-based routing)
- Work assignment (expert receives assets + brand context + instructions)
- Quality control (internal review before delivery)
- Delivery & approval (user reviews, approves, or requests changes)
- Payment processing (tier-based pricing, auto-charge on approval)

*RL Feedback Loop:*

- Edit analysis (compare before/after, extract patterns)
- Pattern storage (in Brand Memory: "User prefers X")
- AI bias generation (influence next generation with learned preferences)
- Quality scoring (track AI improvement over time)

*Expert Management:*

- Expert onboarding (vet, train, category assignment)
- Availability tracking (active/offline status)
- Quality scoring (track approval rates, revision rates)
- Payment distribution (escrow, release on approval)

*Integration Points:*

- Files library (source inputs, store outputs)
- Brand Memory (provide context to experts, store learnings)
- Spaces/Plans ("Refine" button on all outputs)

### Out of Scope (Phase 2)

## 5. CORE REQUIREMENTS

### User Request Flow

1. User selects outputs to refine (from Spaces/Plans/Files)
2. "Refine" button opens request form
3. Form fields:
    - Instructions (text, 2000 char max)
    - Urgency (Standard 24h or Rush 4h)
    - Budget tier (Quick Polish, Creative Direction, Full Production)
    - Add references (optional)
4. Brand context auto-loads (from Brand Memory)
5. Price calculated and shown (tier + urgency + asset count)
6. User confirms, payment authorized (charge on approval)
7. Request enters queue

### Expert Matching

1. Routing algorithm analyzes request:
    - Asset type (image, copy, video)
    - Category (apparel, home decor, tech, food, etc.)
    - Urgency (standard or rush)
2. Match to available expert:
    - Category match (primary)
    - Availability (active, <3 active jobs)
    - Quality score (>4.5 stars, >85% approval rate)
3. Assign to expert, send notification
4. Expert has 2 hours to accept (or auto-reassign)

### Expert Work Environment

1. Expert dashboard shows assigned jobs
2. Each job includes:
    - Original assets (download)
    - Brand context (colors, fonts, style guide)
    - User instructions
    - Reference library (top performers)
    - Estimated time (based on tier)
3. Expert edits offline (using their tools)
4. Expert uploads refined assets + notes
5. Internal QC review (before user delivery)
6. If QC fails → Expert revises
7. If QC passes → Delivered to user

### Quality Control (Internal)

1. Every delivery reviewed by ShopOS QC team (or senior expert)
2. QC checklist:
    - Instructions followed? ✓
    - Brand consistency? ✓
    - Technical quality (resolution, format)? ✓
    - Scope appropriate (no under/over-delivery)? ✓
3. If passes → Deliver
4. If fails → Return to expert with notes (doesn't count against SLA)

### User Approval Flow

1. User receives notification: "Your Refine is ready"
2. Review interface:
    - Side-by-side comparison (original vs refined)
    - Expert notes visible
    - Download refined assets
3. User actions:
    - *Approve:* Payment released, assets saved to Files
    - *Request Changes:* Provide feedback, expert revises (unlimited in scope)
    - *Reject:* Full refund, assets not saved (rare, <5%)
4. After approval:
    - Rate expert (1-5 stars)
    - Assets organized in Files (tagged "refined")
    - RL analysis begins

### RL Feedback Loop

1. After user approval, compare before/after:
    - *Visual edits:* Color shifts, composition changes, contrast adjustments
    - *Copy edits:* Tone shifts, structure changes, word choice patterns
2. Extract patterns:
    - Quantifiable ("Warmed tones +15%")
    - Categorical ("Tighter crops preferred")
    - Confidence-scored (based on consistency across edits)
3. Store in Brand Memory:
    - "User's apparel brand prefers warm tones, tight crops, high contrast"
4. Bias next AI generation:
    - Increase probability of warm tones in color palette
    - Default to tighter crops in composition
    - Boost contrast in post-processing
5. Track quality improvement:
    - Measure: % of outputs needing refinement
    - Target: 20% reduction in 3 months

## 6. BEHAVIORS & RULES

### Pricing Structure

*Tier 1: Quick Polish* ($50-150 per asset)

- Minor edits (color correction, cropping, brightness)
- Delivery: 24h standard, 4h rush (+50%)
- Use case: "90% there, just needs minor tweaks"

*Tier 2: Creative Direction* ($200-500 per asset)

- Major edits (composition, brand consistency, copy rewrite)
- Delivery: 24h standard, 4h rush (+50%)
- Use case: "Good foundation, needs expert direction"

*Tier 3: Full Production* ($500-1000 per asset)

- Complex work (multi-asset coordination, strategy layer)
- Delivery: 48h standard, 8h rush (+50%)
- Use case: "Hero campaign, need full creative treatment"

*Bulk discount:*

- 5-10 assets: -10%
- 11-20 assets: -20%
- 21+ assets: -30%

### SLA Guarantees

*Standard:*

- Quick Polish: 24 hours
- Creative Direction: 24 hours
- Full Production: 48 hours

*Rush:*

- Quick Polish: 4 hours
- Creative Direction: 4 hours
- Full Production: 8 hours
- Cost: +50%

*If SLA missed:*

- Auto-discount: -25% final price
- User notified with new ETA
- Expert quality score impacted

### Revision Policy

- *Within scope:* Unlimited revisions (instructions followed, quality maintained)
- *Out of scope:* New request required (instructions changed, new assets added)
- Example:
    - In scope: "Tones still too cool, warm +5% more"
    - Out of scope: "Actually, change the entire background"

### Expert Quality Scoring

*Tracked metrics:*

- Approval rate (% of deliveries approved first try)
- Avg rating (user 1-5 stars)
- SLA adherence (% delivered on time)
- Revision rate (% requiring changes)

*Quality tiers:*

- Elite (>4.8★, >95% approval, >98% SLA): Priority matching, bonuses
- Good (4.5-4.8★, >85% approval, >90% SLA): Standard matching
- Watch (4.0-4.5★, >70% approval, >80% SLA): Reduced matching, coaching
- Off-boarded (<4.0★ or <70% approval): Removed from pool

### Learning Loop Behavior

*Pattern extraction:*

- Minimum sample: 3 refinements before declaring pattern
- Confidence threshold: >75% to influence AI generation
- Contradictory patterns: Higher weight on recent edits

*AI bias generation:*

- Subtle influence (10-20% probability shift)
- Exploration maintained (30% of generation ignores bias)
- User can override ("Ignore past refinements, fresh approach")

*Quality measurement:*

- Baseline: % of outputs refined in Month 1
- Target: 20% reduction by Month 3
- If no improvement: Review pattern extraction quality

## 7. TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS & EDGE CASES

### Performance Requirements

- Request submission: <2 seconds
- Expert matching: <5 minutes
- Asset upload/download: <30 seconds for 50MB
- Approval flow: <1 second to process

### Scalability Constraints

- Max assets per request: 20
- Max file size: 50MB per asset
- Max experts per category: 50
- Max concurrent jobs per expert: 3

### Expert Pool Management

*Categories needed (launch):*

- Apparel (10 experts)
- Home Decor (8 experts)
- Tech/Electronics (8 experts)
- Food/Beverage (6 experts)
- Beauty/Cosmetics (6 experts)

*Backup coverage:*

- Each category needs 2x coverage (peak demand)
- Rush requests prioritized (pull from standard queue)

### Error Handling

*1. No Expert Available:*

- Queue request, notify user: "High demand. Expected match in 4 hours."
- Offer auto-upgrade to rush (if standard request)
- Refund if >24h delay

*2. Expert Doesn't Accept:*

- Auto-reassign after 2 hours
- Cycle through 3 experts max
- If all decline → Escalate to internal team

*3. QC Fails:*

- Expert revises (doesn't count against user SLA)
- If 2x QC fails → Reassign to different expert

*4. User Rejects Delivery:*

- Investigate reason (expert error vs scope creep)
- If expert error → Full refund, expert scored down
- If scope creep → Offer revision (in scope) or new request (out of scope)

### Edge Cases

*1. Ambiguous Instructions:*

- Expert asks for clarification (via async message)
- SLA paused until user responds
- If no response in 12h → Auto-cancel, refund

*2. Brand Context Missing:*

- Expert proceeds with best judgment
- Flag to user: "No brand guidelines found. Recommend adding to Brand Memory."

*3. Reference Assets Low Quality:*

- Expert ignores, works from instructions
- Note to user: "Reference assets were low resolution, couldn't use."

*4. Scope Creep During Revision:*

- System detects: new instructions != original instructions
- Prompt user: "This appears out of scope. Create new request?"

## 10. ASSUMPTIONS

*We assume:*

1. Expert pool can be recruited and trained within 2 weeks
2. Experts use their own tools (Photoshop, etc.) - we don't provide editing software
3. Users understand "refinement" vs "redesign" (scope boundaries)
4. 24h turnaround acceptable for most use cases (Rush for urgent)
5. Brand Memory provides sufficient context (colors, fonts, style)
6. Users trust black-box expert matching (no expert selection)
7. Payment on approval model is acceptable (not upfront)
8. RL feedback loop can extract meaningful patterns from edits

*We do NOT assume:*

1. All refinements will improve quality (some may fail QC)
2. Users will accept first delivery every time (revisions expected)
3. Experts available 24/7 (need shift coverage)
4. AI will improve linearly (learning may plateau)
5. Users will always provide clear instructions (ambiguity expected)
