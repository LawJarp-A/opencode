# Plans PRD

## What It Is

**Plans** are pre-built workflow chains that transform brand intent into complete deliverable packs.

Not individual tools. Not manual sequencing. **End-to-end execution in a single click.**

User states goal → Plan executes 5-15 spaces automatically → Complete outcome delivered.

## The Core Problem

**Brands don't think in workflows. They think in outcomes.**

Today's reality:

- User wants to "launch a product"
- ShopOS offers 40+ individual Spaces
- User must manually: discover → sequence → execute → compile
- Result: Confusion, incomplete work, abandonment

**The gap:** Our Workflow Repository solves discrete tasks brilliantly. But brands work in **campaigns, launches, seasons**—not isolated steps.

They need **deliverable packs**, not individual outputs.

---

## The Solution: Plans

Plans are **curated execution paths** for common brand workflows.

**The Architecture:**

- **Input:** Single unified form (one-time data collection)
- **Execution:** Planning Mode chains 5-15 spaces sequentially or in parallel
- **Output:** Complete deliverable pack (organized, deployment-ready)
- **Experience:** One click → One progress bar → Done

**The Difference:**

`Spaces     = Tools (hammer, screwdriver, wrench)
Plans      = Blueprints (build a chair, renovate a kitchen)`

User gives intent. Plan delivers outcome.

---

## Position in Product Hierarchy

`Spaces             →  Individual tasks, fast execution
Plans               →  Pre-built paths for common outcomes
Planning Mode       →  Agentic, custom workflow generation`

Plans sit between quick tools and full AI planning. They're **proven playbooks** that encode years of eCommerce expertise.

## Launch Plans (First 4)

### Plan 1: New Product Launch

**User Intent:** *"I have a new product. Get me ready to sell everywhere."*

**Execution Chain:**

**① Catalog Creation** (20-30 assets)

- Studio shots: White background, multiple angles (front, back, side, detail)
- Lifestyle shots: Model or flat lay, 3 variations
- Creative shots: Editorial, seasonal, campaign-style
- Video: 15s product demo + 30s lifestyle cut

**② Marketplace Adaptation**

- Amazon: Resize to specs, add infographics, compliance text
- Etsy: Optimized thumbnails, lifestyle focus
- Website: High-res hero images, gallery pack

**③ Marketing Assets**

- Ad creatives: 10 variations (static + video) for Meta/Google
- Social content: 5 Instagram posts, 3 TikTok hooks
- Email header + product feature banner

**④ Copy & Metadata**

- Product title + description (SEO-optimized)
- Feature bullets (Amazon, Shopify, Etsy formats)
- Ad copy (5 headlines, 10 variations)
- Suggested tags and categories

**Input Requirements:** Product photos (raw), specs, brand context, launch channels, timeline

**Output:** Organized folder structure (`/catalog`, `/marketplace`, `/ads`, `/social`, `/copy`) with 20-30 deployment-ready files

**Time:**

- Manual today: 2-4 weeks
- Plan: **45 minutes**

### Plan 2: Seasonal Campaign Refresh

**User Intent:** *"Holiday season. I need new creatives for all my hero products."*

**Execution Chain:**

**① Trend Intelligence**

- Scrape trending themes (TikTok, Pinterest, Instagram)
- Identify color palettes, design trends, messaging hooks
- Match to brand tone and product category

**② Campaign Creative Generation**

- 15 ad variations per product (static + video)
- Seasonal overlays (holiday props, backgrounds, lighting)
- 3 creative styles: Bold, Minimal, Editorial

**③ Social Content Pack**

- 20 Instagram posts (grid + stories)
- 10 TikTok-style videos (15s, 30s, 60s)
- 5 Pinterest pins

**④ Ad Copy Variants**

- Headlines, CTAs, body copy
- A/B test framework pre-configured

**⑤ Deployment**

- Push to Meta Ads Manager (draft campaigns)
- Schedule to Shopify (homepage banners, collection pages)
- Export social pack

**Input Requirements:** Existing product catalog, campaign theme (e.g., "Holiday 2025 — Cozy Luxe"), channels, budget, key products

**Output:** 100+ assets organized by channel, pre-configured A/B test structure, deployment-ready

**Time:**

- Manual: 3-6 weeks
- Plan: **2 hours**

### Plan 3: Marketplace Expansion

**User Intent:** *"I sell on Shopify. I want to launch on Amazon, Etsy, and Faire."*

**Execution Chain:**

**① Asset Adaptation**

- Resize all product images to marketplace specs
- Amazon: Add infographics, compliance badges, dimension callouts
- Etsy: Lifestyle-heavy, artisan aesthetic
- Faire: B2B positioning, wholesale messaging

**② Copy Rewrite**

- Amazon: Keyword-stuffed, feature-focused, A+ content
- Etsy: Story-driven, handmade emphasis
- Faire: Bulk pricing, retailer benefits, case packs

**③ SEO & Tags**

- Generate marketplace-specific keywords
- Category mapping
- Suggested pricing adjustments per platform

**④ Listing Creation**

- Generate CSV/XML feeds for bulk upload
- API integration where available
- Quality checks (image resolution, character limits)

**Input Requirements:** Existing Shopify catalog, target marketplaces, brand positioning adjustments

**Output:** Marketplace-ready product feeds, asset packs by platform, listing drafts for review

**Time:**

- Manual: 4-8 weeks
- Plan: **3 hours**

---

### Plan 4: Competitor Response Sprint

**User Intent:** *"My competitor just launched a killer campaign. I need to respond fast."*

**Execution Chain:**

**① Competitor Analysis**

- Scrape competitor campaign (ads, landing pages, social)
- Identify messaging, design patterns, hooks
- Highlight gaps or weaknesses

**② Counter-Positioning**

- Generate differentiated messaging
- Suggest creative angles that contrast or outflank
- Recommend product features to emphasize

**③ Rapid Creative Generation**

- 20 ad variations (designed to stand out vs. competitor style)
- 10 social posts
- 3 landing page concepts

**④ Speed Deployment**

- Push ads to Meta/Google (draft)
- Export social pack
- Create A/B test structure

**⑤ Performance Tracking**

- Set up dashboards comparing your campaign vs. competitor benchmarks

**Input Requirements:** Competitor name or campaign URL, your product catalog, budget, timeline

**Output:** Counter-campaign assets, deployment-ready ads, competitive intelligence report

**Time:**

- Manual: 2-4 weeks
- Plan: **4 hours**

## Why Plans Matter

### For Users

**Mental model alignment:** Brands think in outcomes, not processes

- "Launch my product" not "run 12 sequential spaces"
- "Refresh my holiday campaign" not "figure out workflow dependencies"

**Dramatic time compression:**

- Weeks → Hours
- Multiple tools → One click
- Manual assembly → Auto-compiled deliverable packs

**Confidence through proven paths:**

- No guessing about sequence
- No missing steps
- Embedded best practices

### For ShopOS

**GTM velocity:**

- Plans ARE the demo: "Watch us launch your product in 45 minutes"
- Instant proof of value
- Clear differentiation vs. point solutions

**Retention driver:**

- Plans map to recurring brand needs (launches, campaigns, seasonal refreshes)
- Weekly/monthly usage patterns
- High engagement = low churn

**Natural Refine™ funnel:**

- Plans execute tactics
- Refine™ handles strategy
- "Review this campaign before I deploy $10K in ads" → perfect handoff

**Defensible moat:**

- Plans encode years of eCommerce domain expertise
- Sequence optimization = compounding advantage
- Hard to replicate (requires both technical + domain knowledge)

### For Product Development

**Fast iteration:**

- Plans use existing Spaces as building blocks
- Workflow team ships Plans without deep eng/design work
- JSON-based configuration (no code changes)
