# 🔥 UNIVERSAL RED TEAM STRUCTURE

*Every team member: 3 customer interviews per week*

## Interview Framework (30 min each)

1. *Warm-up (5 min):* Who are you? What's your business? What's your current workflow?
2. *Problem exploration (10 min):* Show me what breaks. Walk me through your last project. What took longest?
3. *Prototype test (10 min):* Here's what we're building. Try this. Talk out loud. Where did you get stuck?
4. *Wrap-up (5 min):* What would make this a no-brainer? What would make you pay? What's missing?

## Documentation (15 min post-interview)

- Record session (with permission)
- Write 1-pager: Name, company, key quote, top 3 insights, action items
- Share in Slack #red-team channel
- Tag relevant team members

## Weekly Synthesis (Every Friday)

- Compile 3 interviews into trends doc
- Share with full team
- Highlight patterns across interviews
